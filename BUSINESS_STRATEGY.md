## 1. Executive Summary

GrowWise is a **household-first, AI-native budgeting platform** built on a modern stack (React 19, Express 5, Prisma 5, Google Gemini 2.5). It already has **15+ production features**, a full admin panel, PWA support, gamification, and PostHog analytics — making it significantly ahead of a typical MVP.

The Mint shutdown (2024) left a **$1.57 billion market** without a clear leader for collaborative household finance. GrowWise fills this gap with AI-powered categorization, voice input, and a unique household-centric model that naturally drives viral growth.

**This document covers:** Growth Strategy → SEO → UI/UX Improvements → Regional Pricing → Investment Readiness → Revenue Projections.

## 4. SEO Strategy

### 4.1 Current State

The app is a **React SPA** (Client-Side Rendered), which means:
- ❌ Google crawls it but with delayed indexing
- ❌ No dynamic meta tags per page
- ❌ No Open Graph tags for social sharing
- ❌ No sitemap.xml or robots.txt
- ✅ Public pages exist (Landing, Features, Contact, Privacy, Terms)
- ✅ Fast build with Vite (good Core Web Vitals)

### 4.2 SEO Action Plan

| Priority | Action | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| 🔴 **Critical** | Add `react-helmet-async` for per-page `<title>` and `<meta>` tags | Google indexing |
| 🔴 **Critical** | Add Open Graph + Twitter Card tags | Social sharing previews |
| 🟡 **High** | Generate `sitemap.xml` (auto from routes) | Crawl discovery |
| 🟡 **High** | Add `robots.txt` allowing public pages, blocking `/dashboard`, `/admin` | Crawl control |
| 🟡 **High** | Add JSON-LD structured data (SoftwareApplication schema) | Rich snippets |
| 🟢 **Medium** | Pre-render public pages with `vite-plugin-prerender` | Instant indexing |
| 🟢 **Medium** | Add canonical URLs to prevent duplicate content | SEO hygiene |
| 🟢 **Medium** | Create `/blog` section (static markdown → HTML) | Long-tail traffic |

## 6. Pricing Model (Regional, PPP-Adjusted)

### 6.1 Pricing Philosophy

> **"Freemium with AI as the paywall"**

The app already has `ai_request_count` and `is_ai_restricted` fields in the database. This is the natural paywall — AI features are the premium differentiator, while basic budgeting remains free.

|---|---|---|---|---|

### 6.4 Why This Pricing Works

| Insight | Reasoning |
|---|---|
| **India at ₹149** | India's SaaS tolerance is ₹100-300/month. Competitors like ET Money Pro charge ₹149. Massive TAM of 300M+ households. |
| **USA at $6.99** | YNAB charges $14.99. Positioning at ~50% of YNAB captures price-sensitive users who want AI features. |
| **UK at £5.99** | UK has the highest fintech adoption in Europe. Monzo/Starling users are accustomed to premium tiers at £5-10. |
| **Canada at C$7.99** | Similar dynamics to USA but adjusted for CAD purchasing power. |
| **Middle East at AED 24.99** | UAE/Saudi have high disposable income and large expat families. Growing fintech adoption (50%+ in UAE). Family remittance tracking is a killer feature here. |
| **Annual = ~30% discount** | Drives commitment, reduces churn, improves cash flow predictability. |
